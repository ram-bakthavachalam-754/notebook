Explore — prototype

A new Money section that keeps a master list of every company in the S&P 500, the Nasdaq-100 and five tech indexes (semiconductors, two cybersecurity, cloud software, FANG+), grouped by sector, industry and sub-industry. Pick a list at the top of the middle column. Click a heat map tile or a row on the left to drill down; click a stock for its ticker page. The companies, their groups, prices, 1D changes and market caps are real (close of Oct 26, 2026); the other periods are made up.

Explore

How the app builds the list

  • S&P 500: Wikipedia's “List of S&P 500 companies” has every member with its sector and sub-industry. Explore reads it when first opened and again each week.
  • Nasdaq-100: Nasdaq's own list of members (the same request also returns prices and market caps).
  • Industries: the sector → industry → sub-industry tree is the published standard (GICS), built into the app. It rarely changes.
  • Tech indexes: Semiconductors (SOX), Cybersecurity (CIBR's index) and Cloud Software (WCLD's index) come from Nasdaq's index site; Cyber Security (ISE) from Amplify's daily HACK holdings; FANG+ from its ETN's holdings. Shares listed only abroad (Trend Micro, Thales, Atos) are left out. The app checks each member's name as well as its ticker, since Atos's ticker ATO is also Atmos Energy's in the US.
  • The 90 companies outside the S&P 500 (ASML, Cloudflare, Zscaler, Snowflake…) have no published group, so your AI model places each one once (AI). You can move any of them.
  • When a list changes, Explore notes who joined and who left, e.g. “Added RDDT, removed …”.

The tree

  • S&P 500: 503 listings (500 companies) in 11 sectors, 69 industries and 126 sub-industries.
  • Nasdaq-100: 101 listings (100 companies), 86 of them also in the S&P 500. It leaves out banks and insurers, so PayPal is its only Financials stock and it has no Real Estate.
  • Two share classes of one company count once (GOOGL with GOOG, FOXA with FOX, NWSA with NWS), so Alphabet isn't twice the size on the map.
  • 593 companies across all seven lists; a company in several lists is one entry, and its rows show every list it's in.
  • Top 5 keeps the five largest companies in each sub-industry: the leaders.
  • The tech lists (10–64 stocks) open straight to their stocks, grouped by sub-industry.
  • Left out: IGV (iShares doesn't let apps download its holdings), XLK (the same as S&P 500 › Information Technology) and the Nasdaq Composite (3,000+ stocks).

Heat map

  • Color is the change over the chosen period; size is market cap, or equal sizes.
  • Each level: All → sector (industries) → industry (sub-industries) → sub-industry (stocks) → ticker page. Back (⌘[) goes up.
  • A group's change is the cap-weighted average of its companies.

Reused from Finance and Trading

  • 1D prices from Nasdaq's quote list or Trading's Yahoo batch quotes (TradingModel.quotes), refreshed every few minutes while Explore is open and the market is open.
  • 1W–1Y changes from the cached price history (FinanceModel.cachedPriceHistory), fetched in the background for all ~520 stocks and kept for a day.
  • A stock opens the existing ticker page (TickerDetail). Badges come from your holdings and the Trading watchlist.
