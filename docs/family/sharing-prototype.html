Sharing in Notebook

Two people, two private notebooks. Share a note or a links collection and you can both edit it. Each of you tracks your own finance accounts. Share one and it shows up in the other person's Finance as a read-only shared account. Try sharing “Grocery list” or your Brokerage account from the Mac, then look at your partner's iPhone.
